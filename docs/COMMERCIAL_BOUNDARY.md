# Open-Core and Commercial Boundary

## Open core

The public project should retain the components needed to inspect reliability:

- schemas;
- benchmark cases and expert rubrics;
- claim/failure taxonomy;
- Skills;
- deterministic analytics;
- provider interfaces and reference adapters;
- graders;
- reproducible runner;
- sample reports.

## Private or commercial layer

A commercial system may add:

- Strava/Garmin/FIT/TCX ingestion and account infrastructure;
- consent and retention workflows;
- coach roster and team management;
- proprietary preference labels and production recommendation policy;
- athlete messaging, PDF delivery, billing, and CRM;
- private knowledge bases;
- operational monitoring and support.

## Trust contract

Commercial claims should point back to the exact public benchmark and released model/Skill configuration. The public benchmark must not be changed privately to produce favorable results.
