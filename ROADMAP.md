# Roadmap

## v0.1 — runnable open-core MVP

- 30 cases, one Skill, rules/OpenAI/replay providers, deterministic graders, reports, private longitudinal track.

## v0.2 — real coaching operations

- weekly snapshot diff;
- executed-versus-planned comparison;
- coach review annotations;
- anonymization assistant with human approval;
- report templates for athlete delivery;
- imported structured output from StravaStats.

## v0.3 — evaluation depth

- model-as-judge adapter validated against human grades;
- pairwise preference review;
- failure registry and regression dashboard;
- cost/latency recording without hard-coded vendor prices;
- repeated-run variance analysis.

## v0.4 — broader endurance coverage

- cycling, swimming, triathlon, trail, and marathon-specific case packs;
- environment and altitude packs;
- strength-integration cases;
- multilingual cases and grader equivalence tests.

## v1.0 — stable public protocol

- 100+ expert-reviewed cases;
- external contributors and independent reviewer process;
- stable claim taxonomy and migration policy;
- published model/agent reports;
- production connector boundary for commercial implementations.
