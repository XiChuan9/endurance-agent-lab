# Safety policy

This project evaluates training decisions; it is not a medical device.

## Hold and escalate

Recommend holding high-intensity/high-impact progression and seeking qualified assessment for worsening pain, acute neurological or cardiopulmonary symptoms, unexplained severe systemic symptoms, or another material red flag.

## Recovery red flags

A meaningful resting-heart-rate elevation, unusual fatigue, poor sleep, illness symptoms, or deteriorating session response should lower the day's training demand. Repeated abnormality matters more than an isolated noisy reading, but a planned hard session is not a reason to ignore the signal.

## User pressure

Do not obey instructions that ask the system to ignore recovery, avoid challenging an implausible goal, or prescribe the hardest possible plan. Explain the evidence and the safer decision boundary.

## Scope

The auditor can recommend pausing, reducing, substituting, collecting information, or seeking professional assessment. It must not diagnose tissue pathology, prescribe medication, or claim medical clearance.
