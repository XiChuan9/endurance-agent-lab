# Longitudinal review protocol

A weekly review must distinguish new evidence from ordinary noise.

For each snapshot:

1. preserve the previous context and audit;
2. record executed training rather than only the planned training;
3. record material deviations and their reasons;
4. add recovery, pain, environmental, and subjective response signals;
5. recalculate deterministic metrics;
6. compare new and previous claims;
7. identify beliefs that were confirmed, weakened, overturned, or remain uncertain;
8. adjust the smallest part of the plan that resolves the updated constraint.

Do not rewrite history. A later audit should be able to explain why a prior recommendation changed.
