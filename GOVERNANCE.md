# Governance

The project begins with a lead-maintainer model. Maintainers are responsible for releases, benchmark semantic versioning, privacy review, claim-taxonomy stability, security response, and final merge decisions.

As adoption grows, domain reviewers and engineering maintainers may be added based on sustained, high-quality contributions. Benchmark changes require both engineering validation and domain review.

Commercial users do not receive authority over public benchmark outcomes. Sponsorship and grants must be disclosed when they could create a perceived conflict.
